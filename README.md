# ComputerGraphicsLecture

Wednesday Lab DropBox:
I chose to use the Hologram Shader as I wanted to create a similar look to what your ghost car would look like in a time trial racing game. My goal was to make the hologram have a line speed that helps get the players attention, as the main purpose of the Hologram Shader, would be to create a ghost that helps direct you towards certain areas like a tutorial. I changed the line speed time to sin to give an up and down frequency towards the lines appearance, allowing it to stand out a bit more to the player. I made sure the line color was a brighter white, while everything else was darker to make the line stand out more. I increased the frequency of the line to 10 and made the speed steady enough for the player to tell what's happening but not to slow that it doesn't draw attention anymore.

I wanted to keep the adjustments relatively simple and mainly create a base to what I want, so it could be looked at later and add smaller details.
